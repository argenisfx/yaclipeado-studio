Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

1. Who we are

Ya Clipeado Studio ("the app") is a personal desktop tool operated by the owner of the Ya Clipeado (@yaclipeado) accounts. Only the owner uses it, with his own accounts.

2. Data the app accesses

  • Basic profile information of the owner's own account (identifier, display name, avatar), as returned by the platform login.
  • The list of public videos of the owner's own account and their statistics: views, likes, comments and shares.

The app does not access data of other users, private messages, contacts or payment information.

3. How the data is used

Only to show the owner a ranking and a history of the performance of his own videos.

4. Where the data is stored

On the owner's own computer, in a local database. The access tokens are stored locally in a file readable only by the owner. Nothing is uploaded to external servers.

5. Sharing

The data is not sold, rented or shared with third parties.

6. Retention and deletion

The owner can delete the local database and the tokens at any time, and can revoke the app's access from the settings of each platform (for TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Manage app permissions).

7. Children

The app is not directed to children and does not collect data from them.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated. The current version is always published on this page.

9. Contact

@yaclipeado on TikTok, Instagram, YouTube and Facebook.

Política de Privacidad

Ya Clipeado Studio es una herramienta personal del dueño de las cuentas Ya Clipeado. Solo accede al perfil básico y a la lista de videos públicos de su propia cuenta con sus estadísticas (vistas, me gusta, comentarios y compartidos). Los datos y los tokens se guardan únicamente en su computadora, no se suben a servidores externos y no se venden ni se comparten con terceros. El dueño puede borrar los datos locales y revocar el acceso de la app cuando quiera desde los ajustes de cada plataforma.
